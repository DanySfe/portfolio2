DISCOVER

Problem & Solution

Understanding the challenges and creating solutions that truly matter

The Problem

In today's smart home market, users often own multiple devices from different brands, each requiring its own app to control. This creates confusion, inconsistency, and frustration.

  • • Too many apps for different devices
  • • Inconsistent design and controls
  • • Time-consuming to switch between apps
  • • No unified dashboard for quick access
  • • Limited cross-brand compatibility

The Solution

SyncHaus brings all smart devices together in one universal app, offering a seamless and consistent experience across all your home systems.

  • • One central app for all smart devices
  • • Compatible with multiple brands
  • • Unified dashboard for monitoring
  • • Real-time device feedback
  • • Simple setup with clear navigation

STRATEGY

Design Strategy

Intention

To design a universal smart home platform that lets users control all their connected devices — no matter the brand — in one unified app.

Target User

Homeowners or renters with multiple smart devices from different brands who seek a single app to manage them all.

Tech Constraint

Ensuring stable connectivity and compatibility across various brands via multi-brand API integration and OS differences.
